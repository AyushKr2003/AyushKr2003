02 · Caelestia v1 → niri · 171★

                  
niri-caelestia-shell

                  

                    A port of the original Caelestia shell (v1, built for Hyprland) to the niri compositor, building on jutraim's port. I added a visual config editor, a battery monitor, a smarter workspace bar, CPU/GPU monitoring, niri IPC controls and launcher modes for clipboard, OCR and Google Lens. Drawers grow out of the frame exactly as they do in v1. Archived since I moved to Hyprland.
                  

                  
  • QML
  • Quickshell
  • niri IPC
  • Wayland

03 · web3 · team project

                  
NexVote

                  

                    A voting app where every ballot is an Ethereum transaction. Voters connect a MetaMask wallet, browse elections and vote. The Flutter web client calls castVote on a Solidity contract through web3dart and reads the results back from the chain. I built the Flutter app.
                  

                  
  • Flutter Web
  • web3dart
  • Solidity
  • Node.js

06 · Caelestia v2 → Omarchy · 46★

                  
omacale

                  

                    The current Caelestia (v2, Material 3 Expressive) ported 1:1 to Omarchy as one bar plugin, with no extra daemon and no build step. Its SDF shader merges the frame and every drawer into one gooey surface that stretches on a spring as it moves, and the Material 3 colours regenerate from the wallpaper. The surface here runs a WebGL port of that shader.
                  

                  
  • QML
  • Omarchy
  • Hyprland
  • GLSL
